Grammar
<p>
                <!---->
                Past Perfect <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                - used to make it clear that one past action happened before another past action
                - form = had + Past Participle
                - sentence formation = action before past action (his heart had stopped) + past action after previous action (By the time they got to him)
                - "By the time they got to him, his heart had stopped." 
                - formation
                - Affirmative sentence
                - I/he/she/it/you/we/they + had + watched TV.
                - Negative sentence
                - I/he/she/it/you/we/they + had not + watched TV.
                - Yes/No questions
                - Had + I/he/she/it + watching TV?              Yes, I/he/she/it/you/we/they had/No, I/he/she/it/you/we/they had not
                - Wh- questions
                - What + had + I/he/she/it/you/we/they + watched?
                - Subject questions
                - Who + had + watched TV?

                <!---->
                Reported Speech <br> (OVERVIEWE)
                - to report what other people say, we either use Direct Speech (we quote someones actual words) or reported speech
                - verb forms and pronouns change depending on the context
                - we often use say (that), tell somebody (that) and indirect objects (me, us, etc.)
                - "She told me/said (that) the match was great."
                - "He told me that he had felt dizzy."
                - Tenses changes

<p>
                <!---->
                Phrasal verbs <br>(OVERVIEWED)
                - formed by a main verb and preposition or adverb
                - look (main verb) + for (preposition) = look for
                - Types of phrasal verbs
                - without an object (join in, work out, fit in, pass out)
                - "Yesterday was very hot and a few people passed out because of that."
                - with an object after the phrasal verb or between the main verb and preposition
                - we only use pronouns (him, them, it) between the main verb and proposition
                - "I took up handball and rugby."
                - "I took handball and rugby up at the same time."
                - "I took them up at the same time."
                - with an object (both noun and pronoun)
                - "Every spring my brother suffers from an allergy."
                - may also be made of three parts and followed by an object (noun or pronoun)
                - "We look forward to playing basketball in our new court."
                <!---->
                Stuctures with make <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                - make always takes an object and either the infinitive without to, an adjective or a noun
                - make + object + infinitive without to
                - "Make time endless."
                - make + object + adjective/noun
                - "Make turtles non-endangered."
              </p>

● Past Perfect  
   ○ Koristi se da se naglasi kako se jedna radnja u prošlosti dogodila prije druge prošle radnje  
   ○ Tvori se = had + glagol u trećem obliku (Past Participle)  
   ○ Tvorba rečenice = radnja prije prošle radnje (his heart had stopped) + prošla radnja koja se dogodila nakon prethodne (By the time they got to him)  
   ○ Primjer = "By the time they got to him, his heart had stopped."  
   ○ Tvorba  
      ● Potvrdni oblik = I/he/she/it/you/we/they + had + watched TV.  
      ● Niječni oblik = I/he/she/it/you/we/they + had not + watched TV.  
      ● Da/Ne pitanja = Had + I/he/she/it + watching TV?  
         ○ Odgovor = Yes, I/he/she/it/you/we/they had / No, I/he/she/it/you/we/they had not  
      ● Pitanja s upitnim riječima = What + had + I/he/she/it/you/we/they + watched?  
      ● Pitanja na subjekt = Who + had + watched TV?

● Phrasal verbs (frazeološki glagoli)  
   ○ Tvore se od glavnog glagola i prijedloga ili priloga  
      ● look (main verb) + for (preposition) = look for  
   ○ Vrste phrasal verbs  
      ● Bez objekta (neprijelazni) = join in, work out, fit in, pass out  
         ○ Primjer = "Yesterday was very hot and a few people passed out because of that."  
      ● S objektom koji dolazi iza glagola ili između glavnog glagola i prijedloga  
         ○ Zamjenice (him, them, it) moraju doći isključivo između glavnog glagola i prijedloga  
         ○ Primjeri  
            ● "I took up handball and rugby."  
            ● "I took handball and rugby up at the same time."  
            ● "I took them up at the same time."  
      ● S objektom (i imenica i zamjenica mogu doći iza prijedloga)  
         ○ Primjer = "Every spring my brother suffers from an allergy."  
      ● Mogu biti i trodijelni te zahtijevaju objekt iza sebe  
         ○ Primjer = "We look forward to playing basketball in our new court."  

● Strukture s glagolom make  
   ○ Glagol make uvijek zahtijeva objekt, a nakon njega dolazi ili infinitiv bez "to", ili pridjev, ili imenica  
   ○ make + objekt + infinitiv bez "to"  
      ● Primjer = "Make time endless."  
   ○ make + objekt + pridjev/imenica  
      ● Primjer = "Make turtles non-endangered."
