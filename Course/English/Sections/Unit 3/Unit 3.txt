                Comparative and superlative adjectives (OVERVIEWED)
                - ways to compare things and persons 
                - comparative - used to compare two people and things 
                - formation: adjective + than 
                - "Daniel Craig is taller than Zac Efron." 
                - It may be used with following 
                - a bit ("Reading a book is a bit easier.") 
                - much/far ("This is much more expensive because it is more complicated.") 
                - superlative 
                - used to show that person or thing has the highest degree of a certain quality 
                - "Sean Connery is the tallest of the three James Bond actors." 
                - other ways to compare things and persons 
                - (not) as + adjective + as 
                - "Rupert Grint is not as famous as Daniel Radcliffe." 
                - (just) as + adjective + as 
                - used when two people or things are the same 
                - "The plot is as complex as it can be." 
                - not as + adjective + as 
                - used when two people or things are different 
                - "For me, talent shows are not as interesting as game shows."

                Present Perfect with just, already, (not) yet and Past Simple <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                - Present Perfect <br>
                - its usage <br>
                - used to talk about actions which happened and finished in the past, but we do not know when exactly <br>
                - "I have read a lot of biographies and autobiographies." <br>
                - used to talk about actions which happened in the past and the result is visible in the present <br>
                - "Eddie has painted his first potrait." <br>
                - Common time expressions used with Present Perfect <br>
                - expression ever is used in questions <br>
                - "Have you ever been to an art gallery?" <br>
                - expression never is used in negative sentences<br> 
                - "My grandparents have never left England." <br>
                - expression since then <br>
                - "She won The X Factor in 2006. Since then she has sold millions of albums." <br>
                - expressions already and just are used mainly in affirmative sentences between have and the Past Participle verb form <br>
                - "I have already seen it." <br>
                - "They have just left." <br>
                - expression yet is used in negative sentences and questions and always at the end of the sentence <br>
                - "I have not seen her yet." <br>
                - time expression Past Simple is used if we want to say when something happened or with questions with when <br>
                - "She won a Grammy in 2016." <br>
                - "When did you see Blur play live?" <br>

                too and not enough <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                - both are used to make comparisons and talk about a degree of a quality <br>
                - too <br>
                - means "more than you need or want"<br> 
                - its usage <br>
                - before adjectives<br> 
                - "The painting is too dark i cannot see what is in it." <br>
                - with nouns with expressions to many/much <br>
                - "There are too many horror films at the cinema these days." <br>
                - in an affirmative sentence (to create negative meaning) <br>
                - "The plot is too complex." (I cannot understand it) <br>
                - with negation (to create positive meaning) <br>
                - "The plot is not too complex." (It is easy to understand) <br>
                - (not) enough - enough means "the amount you need" <br>
                - "We had enough photos to prepare." <br>
                - not enough means "less than you need or want" <br>
                - "We did not have enough photos to prepare." <br>
                - its usage - after adjectives <br>
                - "The special effects were not realistic enough." <br>
                - before nouns <br>
                - "There are enough cooking programmes on TV nowadays." <br>
                <!---->
                Adjectives to describe films, plots, screenplays etc. <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                - We use <br>
                - superb, brilliant, inspiring to mean fantastic <br>
                - engaiging to mean something pleasant <br>
                - amusing to mean funny <br>
                - thrilling to mean something that makes you excited and makes you want to do something important <br>
                - convincing to mean something which makes you believe something is right <br>
                - predictable to mean somethin not surprising or you expected to happen<br>

                A film review <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                - Background information <br>
                - Directed by Denis Villeneuve<br> 
                - starring Ryan Gosling and Harrison Ford <br>
                - The film was nominated for/was awarded the... <br>
                - This action-adventure/romantic comedy/thriller/historical drama/animated feature film ... <br>
                - The film is a sequel of/a remake of/an adaptation of <br>
                - It is based on the true story of/the novel ... <br>
                - It is set 30 years later/during the etc... <br>
                - The action takes place in the future/in a village/in ... <br>
                - Ryan Gostling/the actor stars as <br>
                - Plot <br>
                - At first/Then/After that/Later on/Unfortunately, etc. <br>
                - Main characters <br>
                - The characters are skillfully played/are not very convincing <br>
                - Harrison Ford gives a great performance as etc. <br>
                - Different aspects of the film <br>
                - The screenplay holds your attention from beginning to end <br>
                - The special effects are amazing <br>
                - The plot is fascinating/a bit boring/slow-moving <br>
                - The photography/soundtrack is amazing <br>
                - Summary of opinion and recommendation <br>
                - For me the word that best describes this movie is etc. <br>
                - It is one of the best science-fiction films i have ever seen <br>
                - In my view, it is suitable for adults and teenagers/kids alike<br>
