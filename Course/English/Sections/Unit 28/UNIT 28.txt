● GRAMMAR

===================================================

● Infinitives
    ○ Infinitiv je osnovni oblik glagola
        ○ Primjer s "to": What seems to worry a lot of people nowadays is the speed of technological change.
        ○ Primjer bez "to": Do you think I could use your laptop for a minute?

● Types of infinitives
    ○ Postoji nekoliko vrsta infinitiva
        ○ Simple infinitives
            ○ Primjer: If you are unable to access a particular website, there might be a problem with your Internet connection.
        ○ Progressive infinitives – (to) be + -ing oblik
            ○ Primjer: He seems to be playing video games all the time.
            ○ Primjer: Before we know it, robots will be teaching our children in schools.
        ○ Perfect infinitives – (to) have + past participle
            ○ Primjer: I meant to have sent you a text, but I forgot.
            ○ Primjer: You should have told me you wanted an e-reader for your birthday.
        ○ Passive infinitives – (to) be + past participle
            ○ Primjer: It remains to be seen whether and how these technological innovations will be used in the future.
        ○ Kombinacije (perfect progressive, perfect passive)
            ○ Primjer (perfect progressive infinitive): He was proven to have been seeing the victim before the murder.
            ○ Primjer (perfect passive infinitive): We should have been taught how to spell without a spell checker.

● Functions of infinitives
    ○ Infinitivi iza glagola
        ○ Neki drugi glagoli iza kojih slijedi infinitiv: afford, agree, appear, ask, choose, claim, decide, expect, fail, happen, hope, intend, learn, manage, mean, offer, prepare, promise, refuse, seem, tend, want, wish
            ○ Primjer: Tom appears to be very knowledgeable about solar installations.
            ○ Primjer: Their child has quickly learnt to use social media.
        ○ Većina modalnih glagola i glagolskih fraza iza sebe zahtijevaju infinitiv bez "to"
            ○ Primjer: I’m not sure, but Peter might have fixed it for you.
            ○ Modalni glagoli iza kojih slijedi infinitiv bez "to": can, could, may, might, must, needn’t, shall, should, will, would.
            ○ Glagolske fraze iza kojih slijedi infinitiv bez "to": would rather/had better
                ○ Primjer: I’d rather explain the matter to him when I see him than discuss it over the phone.
                ○ Primjer: You’d better read the instructions before you try to install the latest updates.
        ○ Neki glagoli iza sebe zahtijevaju objekt + infinitiv
            ○ Primjer: He encouraged me to consider getting the latest plasma TV.
            ○ Primjer: The usher gently reminded us to switch off our mobile phones.
            ○ Neki drugi česti glagoli iza kojih slijedi objekt + infinitiv: advise, allow, ask, beg, cause, enable, expect, persuade, tell, urge, warn.
        ○ "Make" i "let" iza sebe zahtijevaju objekt + infinitiv bez "to"
            ○ Primjer: The joke about robot teachers was really funny – it made everybody laugh.
            ○ Primjer: My older brother never lets me use his devices.

    ○ Infinitivi u frazama iza glagola
        ○ To be + infinitiv kada govorimo o budućim dogovorima ili pravilima
            ○ Primjer: The grand opening of the new research centre is to take place tomorrow.
            ○ Primjer: You are not to use the television set or Smart Board without permission from staff.

    ○ Infinitivi iza nekih pridjeva
        ○ Ovi pridjevi često opisuju osjećaje i reakcije ljudi: anxious, curious, determined, eager, excited, glad, happy, pleased, shocked, surprised, thrilled
            ○ Primjer: I was anxious to start using my new camera right away.
            ○ Primjer: You’ll be shocked to see how technology has changed our sport.
        ○ Ostali česti pridjevi iza kojih može slijediti infinitiv: careful, certain, difficult, fortunate, likely, lucky, right, stupid, sure, unlikely, wrong.

    ○ Infinitivi iza nekih imenica
        ○ Primjer: There is no need to update this software every time a new product is released.
        ○ Primjer: It was a nuisance to have to restart the computer in order to reopen the browser.
        ○ Neke druge imenice iza kojih može slijediti infinitiv: advice, attempt, chance, decision, opportunity, permission, pleasure, reason, refusal, request, way, wish.

    ○ Infinitivi iza superlativa ili rednog broja
        ○ Značenje je slično relativnoj rečenici s who, which ili that
            ○ Primjer: Overall, it is the easiest and the best machine to be found. (= which can be found)
            ○ Primjer: What’s the name of the oldest device to measure time? (= which measured)
            ○ Primjer: Ray Tomlinson was the first to send an email message. (= who sent)

    ○ Infinitiv za izražavanje svrhe
        ○ Možemo koristiti infinitiv da pokažemo zašto netko nešto radi
        ○ Infinitiv se može staviti ispred ili iza subjekta
            ○ Primjer: I read the manual to find out all the settings.
            ○ Primjer: To turn off, you should press and hold the power button.
        ○ Za označavanje svrhe u formalnijem jeziku, također možemo koristiti "in order to" ili "so as to"
            ○ Primjer: We all need adequate training in order to make the best of the IT equipment.
            ○ Primjer: We request all books be returned early so as to make sure none get taken home accidentally.
        ○ "In order to" i "so as to" često se koriste ispred niječnog infinitiva
            ○ Primjer: He kept a diary in order not to forget anything that happened during the trip.
            ○ Primjer: My grandmother wants a tablet so as not to get behind the times.

    ○ Infinitiv kao subjekt rečenice
        ○ Infinitiv se može koristiti kao subjekt, ali uglavnom u formalnom jeziku, definicijama iz rječnika ili citatima
            ○ Primjer: To learn how to use this application is easy.
            ○ Primjer: To explore the Web is time-consuming.
            ○ Primjer: To err is human.
        ○ U svakodnevnom neformalnom engleskom često koristimo "it" kao "pripremni subjekt" na početku, a infinitivnu klauzulu premještamo na kraj
            ○ Primjer: It is easy to learn how to use this application.
            ○ Primjer: It is time-consuming to explore the Web.
            ○ Primjer: It is human to err.

● Sentence modifiers

● Komentiranje s priložnim oznakama rečenice (sentence adverbs and adverbial expressions)
    ○ Komentarski prilozi i priložni izrazi modificiraju cijele klauzule, a ne pojedinačne glagole, pridjeve ili priloge
    ○ Koristimo ih da odrazimo stav govornika/pisca prema informaciji u izjavi, dodajući komentar na sadržaj
    ○ Mogu stajati na početku, u sredini ili na kraju rečenice
        ○ U razgovoru često dolaze na početku ili na kraju
        ○ U pisanju su često odvojeni zarezom, posebno na početnoj ili završnoj poziciji
        ○ Prilozi definitely, possibly i probably obično dolaze u sredini

    ○ Funkcije komentarskih priloga i izraza
        ● Za izražavanje stava/reakcije na radnju ili događaj
            ○ annoyingly, curiously, (un)fortunately, happily, hopefully, interestingly, regrettably, sadly, strikingly, surprisingly, thankfully, understandably, wrongly
            ○ Primjer: Interestingly, the first ever portable MP3 player was developed with funding from the German government.
            ○ Primjer: Fortunately, we’ve all passed our IT Fundamentals exam.

        ● Za ublažavanje poruke ili činjenje manje definitivnom, za smanjenje učinka izjave
            ○ admittedly, apparently, arguably, in a way (spoken), likely, maybe (spoken), possibly, potentially, presumably, probably, sort of (spoken), to some extent
            ○ Primjer: If the result looks too good to be true, it probably is.
            ○ Primjer: Apparently, robins are very aggressive, contrary to popular opinion.
            ○ Primjer: In a way, I’m sorry I didn’t take the job, it might have been interesting.

        ● Za naglašavanje onoga što se želi reći ili reći da je nešto očito
            ○ certainly, clearly, crucially, definitely, doubtless, evidently, indeed, obviously, of course, plainly, surely, undoubtedly, without doubt
            ○ Primjer: Surely, we can’t just stand back and let this happen? There must surely be some explanation.
            ○ Primjer: I was, of course, not very surprised when it turned out my grandmother had no clue how to use her new notebook.

        ● Za ispravljanje neistine ili nesporazuma
            ○ actually, as a matter of fact, in actual fact, in fact, in reality, in truth
            ○ Primjer: Are you disappointed? No, I’m rather glad, actually.
            ○ Primjer: In fact, I am not a computer geek at all!

        ● Za označavanje načina na koji se govori (govorim iskreno/istinito, iz osobne perspektive, itd.)
            ○ frankly, honestly, personally, seriously, to be honest
            ○ Primjer: Frankly, I don’t agree with your argument.
            ○ Primjer: Quite honestly, I can’t imagine reading a book on an e-reader. I think they’re a terrible invention, personally.

        ● Za uvođenje nove teme koja je upravo pala na pamet, ili dodavanje informacija
            ○ actually, by the way (spoken), incidentally, indeed (formal)
            ○ Primjer: I’ve known Jane for years. Since we were at primary school, actually.
            ○ Primjer: I went to see Jessie J’s concert last week. Incidentally/By the way, what do you think of latest album?

        ● Za promjenu teme ili završetak razgovora (govoreni jezik)
            ○ anyway
            ○ Primjer: Anyway, let’s leave that for the moment and talk about it tomorrow.
            ○ Primjer: Anyway, I must be going now.

● Komentiranje s infinitivnim i participnim klauzama
    ○ Možemo koristiti infinitivne i participne klauze za izražavanje stava govornika/pisca ili dodavanje komentara o istinitosti
        ○ to be honest/to tell the truth
            ○ Primjer: To tell the truth, I never believed we would win a gold medal.
            ○ Primjer: I thought we would lose, to be honest.
        ○ all things considered/considering the circumstances/generally speaking
            ○ Primjer: All things considered, the team did very well.
            ○ Primjer: The more expensive the equipment, generally speaking, the better it is.

● Komentiranje s konačnim komentarskim klauzama (finite comment clauses)
    ○ Također možemo koristiti konačne komentarske klauze u govoru
        ○ as you know, I’m afraid, I’m happy/sad/sorry to say, you know, you see
        ○ Primjer: I’m afraid I don’t understand modern technology. I grew up before computers were invented, you see.
        ○ Primjer: My granddaughter is teaching me how to use one, I’m happy to say.

● LANGUAGE FOCUS

===================================================

● Clauses of purpose

    ○ to + infinitiv + imenička fraza
        ○ Primjer: Want something to spice up your run? Use this app to log your activity.

    ○ in order to/so as to + infinitivna klauza
        ○ Primjer: Many people need encouragement (in order) to get off the couch. ("in order" se obično izostavlja u neformalnom pisanju)
        ○ Primjer: This app offers built-in rewards so as to increase your motivation.
        ○ Napomena: Koristite "in order not to/so as not to + infinitiv" za izražavanje svrhe u niječnom obliku
            ○ Primjer: Keep your pedometer in a waist bag in order not to/so as not to lose it.

    ○ so (that) + klauza (obično s modalnim glagolom – can, will ili may)
        ○ Primjer: Get Running offers routines to follow so (that) you can achieve a 5k run.

    ○ for + imenica/-ing
        ○ Primjer: We went for a run.
        ○ Primjer: This app works best for walking.

● WRITING

===================================================

● App Review: Get Running

● Cijeli tekst recenzije
    ○ Want to get fit but finding it hard to get off the couch and hit the streets? With the proliferation of running apps now available, it’s never been easier. Whether you’re training for your first five-kilometre (5k) run or your ninth marathon, there’s an app to motivate you through every mile.
    ○ Get Running is one of the best apps to use the popular training programme Couch to 5k. The method is designed to start you from scratch as it alternates running and walking in order to build up your fitness.
    ○ Get Running not only offers nine weeks of set routines so as to enable beginners to achieve a 5k run in just over two months, but also guides more advanced runners towards increasing their pace.
    ○ The app is easy to use and focuses on regular cues to motivate you, helped by an encouraging voice to talk you through things. As well as enabling you to listen to music while you run, the app allows you to pause a run so that you can get your breath back and start again when you’re ready.
    ○ These are all great features, but it’s the clear and concise instructions that really make all the difference. That said, it would be a real plus if the app was available in a wider range of languages.
    ○ While it won’t do the running for you, this technology can help you move from the couch to the finishing line. If you’re a beginner in need of extra encouragement, this is the perfect choice for you.

● WRITING FOCUS: A review of a product

    ○ Introduction
        ○ Arouse the reader’s interest in the product
        ○ Interested in …?/(1) Want to …?
        ○ (2) you’re … or …

    ○ Main paragraphs
        ○ Say what the product does and what it offers
            ○ It is (3) …
            ○ It enables/ (4) (you) to …
            ○ It has/offers/boasts excellent features such as/like …
        ○ Explain any special benefits or features
            ○ An outstanding feature is …
            ○ This option is particularly useful …
            ○ It’s the (instructions) that really stand out/ (5)
        ○ Use positive adjectives to describe features and benefits
            ○ It’s (6) /user-friendly.
            ○ The (7) (instructions) …
        ○ Use listing phrases to connect the information
            ○ It (8) …, but also …
            ○ (9) (enabling) you to …
            ○ Another advantage is …
        ○ Include a comparison with other similar products if appropriate
            ○ It’s (10) /most popular … available …
            ○ Some may find …, but …
        ○ Mention any drawbacks or areas for improvement
            ○ The only real downside is …
            ○ It would be a real (11) if …

    ○ Conclusion
        ○ Summarise the benefits of the product
            ○ This is a great product to …
            ○ (12) /might not …
            ○ This is (13) for (novices)/the best value …

● SPEAKING

===================================================

● SPEAKING FOCUS

    ● Agreeing enthusiastically
        ○ Absolutely!
        ○ I’m totally with you on that!
        ○ I’ve got to agree with you!
        ○ You can say that again!
        ○ You’re definitely not wrong there!
        ○ You’ve got that right.

    ● Agreeing in part
        ○ You’re right of course but ...
        ○ I know that’s true but ...
        ○ You’ve got a point, though...
        ○ I agree ... but ...
        ○ I guess you’re right, though ...

    ● Disagreeing politely
        ○ Sorry, but I just don’t think that’s right.
        ○ I’m sorry, I really don’t agree with you here.
        ○ I’m afraid I have to disagree with you on that.
        ○ Hmm, I’m afraid I’m not so sure.
