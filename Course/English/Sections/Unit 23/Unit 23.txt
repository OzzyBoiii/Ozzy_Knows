                    Advanced passive forms <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                    Passive forms with modal structures
                    We
                    can use the Passive:
                    ⚫ with modal verbs in the present (modal verb +be+ Past Participle):
                    The project must be completed by tomorrow.
                    Mobile phones may be used as directed by the staff.
                    ⚫ with modal verbs in the past (modal verb + have been + Past Participle):
                    The competition should have been cancelled after the accident. (But it wasn't, and the competition continued). The server couldn't have been repaired properly yesterday. (And it still isn't working properly).
                    Her Facebook profile must have been removed because I can't find it.
                    Passive forms with verbs that take two objects
                    • Some verbs, e.g. ask, bring, buy, give, hand, offer,
                    pay,
                    promise, sell, send, show, teach, tell, etc. can take a direct and an indirect object.
                    They promised a bonus and a pay rise (indirect object) to John (direct object).
                    The pupils gave the teacher (direct object) a bunch of flowers. (indirect object)
                    • Depending on what we want to emphasise, we can form passive statements by beginning with either the director the indirect object:
                    A bonus and a pay rise were promised to John. (indirect object becomes the subject with emphasis on 'a bonus and a pay rise'
                    John was promised a bonus and a pay rise. (direct object becomes the subject with emphasis on 'John')
                    A bunch of flowers was given to the teacher by the pupils. (indirect object becomes the subject with emphasis on 'a bunch of flowers)
                    The teacher was given a bunch of flowers by the pupils. (direct object becomes the subject with emphasis on the teacher).
                    Passive forms with verbs + -ing and verbs + to infinitive •To form the Passive of verbs and phrases followed by a gerund, e.g. be interested in, can't stand, don't mind, enjoy, get tired of, hate, like/dislike, love, miss, etc., we use verb/phrase + being + Past Participle:
                    My IT teacher doesn't mind being asked millions of questions. I got tired of being constantly criticised.
                    •To form the Passive of verbs and phrases followed by
                    to + infinitive, e.g. can't afford, choose, decide, hope, need, profer, pretend, refuse, want, would like, etc., we use
                    verb/phrase + to be + Past Participle:
                    Millions of people hope to be cured of the disease. At some point your son will need to be told the truth.
                    •To form the Passive of the phrase make sb do sth we use form of be+made+ to infinitive:
                    My mother made me clean the bathroom and the kitchen. (active sentence)
                    I was made to clean the bathroom and the kitchen (by my mother) (passive sentence)
                    <!---->
                    Passive reporting structures <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                    To talk about general opinions and views, we use verbs such as agree, believe, daim, consider, expect, feel, hope, know, report, say, think, understand, etc. and the following structures ⚫it+passive verb (present or past) + that
                    People claim that optimists live longer. → It is claimed that optimists live longer.
                    People believed that mathematics was the link between the supernatural and the world of men. → It was believed that mathematics was the link between the supernatural and the world of men.
                    ⚫ subject + passive verb (present or past) + to infinitiva: Computer scientists consider wearable technology to be the future of computing. → Wearable technology is considered to be the future of computing.
                    Doctors know that almonds protected against diabetes. → Almonds were known to protect against diabetes. The infinitive may also be replaced by be + Present/Past Participle:
                    They expect that over three million smartphones will be sold.→ Over three million smartphones are expected to
                    be sold.
                    People thought that the Ministry of Health was preparing new regulations. → The Ministry of Health was thought to be preparing now regulations.
                    ⚫subject + passive verb (present or past) + to have + Past Participle
                    Experts estimate that the earthquake has killed more than 1,000 people. → The earthquake is estimated to have killed more than 1,000 people.
                    The press previously reported that the company's profit had reached £100 million. The company's profit was previously reported to have reached £100 million.
                    ⚫ there + passive verb (present or past) + to be: Analysts expect that there will be a rise in the number of jobs offered across the UK. → There is expected to be a rise in the number of jobs offered across the UK.
                    ⚫ there + passive verb (present or past) + to have been: Historians think that there were over twenty inventors before Edison who invented various light sources. → There are thought to have been over twenty inventors before Edison who invented various light sources.

                    6 let out a long loud breath because you are sad, relieved, or frustrated-  gasp

 <p class="zoomable-text">
                    Structure of a narrative <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                    Short stories are usually written from a first-person point of view (the main character tells the story) or a third-
                    person point of view (the writer tells the story but is not a character in it).
                    Beginning
                    • Get the reader's attention with a dramatic or mysterious opening, a statement by one of the main characters, or a detail concoming an important place or person from
                    the
                    story.
                    • Set the scene. Say where and when the story takes place and introduce the main characters.
                    Middle
                    • Use narrative tenses to describe the action.
                    • Add drama and interest with shorter sentences. • Use language that appeals to the senses to describe what the characters see, hear and feel.
                    • Report speech using a variety of verbs.
                    • Give characters a voice using direct speech.
                    End
                    • Conclude the story with a twist, a lesson leamed, a decision taken (as a result of what happened), or a later event linked to the story.

● Gramatika – napredni pasivni oblici
===================================================
    ○ Pregled
    ○ Pasivni oblici s modalnim strukturama
    ● Pasiv možemo koristiti:
        ○ s modalnim glagolima u sadašnjosti
            ● (modalni glagol + be + glagolska pridjev prošli)
            ● Projekt mora biti dovršen do sutra.
            ● Mobilni telefoni se mogu koristiti prema uputama osoblja.
        ○ s modalnim glagolima u prošlosti
            ● (modalni glagol + have been + glagolska pridjev prošli)
            ● Natjecanje je trebalo biti otkazano nakon nesreće.
            ● Server jučer nije mogao biti popravljen kako treba.
            ● Njezin Facebook profil je sigurno uklonjen jer ga ne mogu pronaći.
===================================================
    ○ Pasivni oblici s glagolima koji imaju dva objekta
        ● Neki glagoli (npr. ask, bring, buy, give, hand, offer, pay, promise, sell, send, show, teach, tell) mogu imati direktni i indirektni objekt.
            ● Obećali su bonus i povišicu Johnu.
            ● Učenici su dali učiteljici buket cvijeća.
        ● Ovisno o tome što želimo naglasiti, možemo formirati pasivne rečenice počevši s direktnim ili indirektnim objektom.
            ● Bonus i povišica su obećani Johnu.
            ● Johnu je obećan bonus i povišica.
            ● Buket cvijeća je dan učiteljici od strane učenika.
            ● Učiteljici je dan buket cvijeća od strane učenika.
===================================================
    ○ Pasivni oblici s glagolima + -ing i glagolima + to infinitiv
        ● Za tvorbu pasiva glagola i fraza nakon kojih slijedi gerund, koristimo glagol/frazu + being + glagolska pridjev prošli.
            ● Mog učitelja informatike ne smeta kad ga se zasipa milijunima pitanja.
            ● Dosadilo mi je da me se stalno kritizira.
        ● Za tvorbu pasiva glagola i fraza nakon kojih slijedi to + infinitiv, koristimo glagol/frazu + to be + glagolska pridjev prošli.
            ● Milijuni ljudi se nadaju da će biti izliječeni od bolesti.
            ● U jednom trenutku će tvoj sin morati da mu se kaže istina.
        ● Za tvorbu pasiva fraze make sb do sth koristimo oblik be + made + to infinitiv.
            ● Moja me mama natjerala da očistim kupaonicu i kuhinju.
            ● Natjerana sam očistiti kupaonicu i kuhinju.
===================================================
    ○ Pasivne strukture za izvještavanje
        ● Za govor o općim mišljenjima i stavovima koristimo glagole kao što su agree, believe, claim, consider, expect, feel, hope, know, report, say, think, understand, itd.
        ● it + pasivni glagol + that
            ● Tvrdi se da optimisti žive duže.
            ● Vjerovalo se da je matematika bila veza između natprirodnog i svijeta ljudi.
        ● subjekt + pasivni glagol + to infinitiv
            ● Smatra se da je nosiva tehnologija budućnost računarstva.
            ● Poznato je da bademi štite od dijabetesa.
        ● Infinitiv može biti zamijenjen s be + sadašnji/prošli particip.
            ● Očekuje se da će biti prodano preko tri milijuna pametnih telefona.
            ● Smatralo se da Ministarstvo zdravstva priprema nove propise.
        ● subjekt + pasivni glagol + to have + glagolska pridjev prošli
            ● Procjenjuje se da je potres ubio više od 1000 ljudi.
            ● Prethodno je objavljeno da je dobit tvrtke dosegnula 100 milijuna funti.
        ● there + pasivni glagol + to be
            ● Očekuje se porast broja ponuđenih poslova diljem UK.
        ● there + pasivni glagol + to have been
            ● Smatra se da je bilo preko dvadeset izumitelja prije Edisona koji su izumili različite izvore svjetlosti.

● Jezik – lako zamjenjive riječi
===================================================
    ○ Pregled
    ● Riječi koje izgledaju ili zvuče slično, ali imaju različita značenja, npr.:
        ○ cite (citirati), site (lokacija), sight (pogled)
        ○ conscious (pri svijesti), conscientious (savjestan)
        ○ dessert (desert), desert (pustinja)
        ○ late (kasno), lately (nedavno)
        ○ principal (glavni/ravnatelj), principle (princip)
        ○ proceed (nastaviti), precede (prethoditi)
        ○ sensible (razuman), sensitive (osjetljiv)
        ○ weather (vrijeme), whether (da li)
        ○ Ravnatelj je razgovarao s roditeljima. Princip vegetarijanstva je ne jesti meso.
    ● Riječi koje izgledaju ili zvuče slično i imaju slična značenja, npr.:
        ○ alike (slično), likely (vjerojatno)
        ○ advise (savjetovati), advice (savjet)
        ○ alone (sam), lone (usamljen), lonely (usamljen i tužan)
        ○ assure (uvjeriti), ensure (osigurati), insure (osigurati)
        ○ breathe (disati), breath (dah)
        ○ dependant (uzdržavanik), dependent (ovisan)
        ○ economic (gospodarski), economical (ekonomičan)
        ○ affect (utjecati), effect (učinak)
        ○ historical (povijesni), historic (povijesno važan)
        ○ lay (položiti), lie (ležati)
        ○ migrate (seliti se), immigrate (useliti se), emigrate (iseliti se)
        ○ raise (podići), rise (porasti), arise (nastati)
        ○ Molim vas, položite svoje telefone na stolove. Moj djed voli ležati na kauču nakon ručka.
    ● Riječi koje izgledaju ili zvuče različito, ali imaju slična značenja, npr.:
        ○ after all (na kraju krajeva), finally (konačno), lastly (naposljetku), eventually (naposljetku)
        ○ comprise (sadržavati), consist of (sastojati se od), include (uključivati)
        ○ occasion (prigoda), opportunity (prilika)
        ○ Na kraju krajeva, jednostavno je. Naposljetku je upalio auto. Naposljetku, dodajte malo soli. Tečaj se sastoji od knjige i CD-a. Tečaj uključuje CD. Bila je to jedina prilika. Kupio sam to za posebnu prigodu.
    ● Lažni prijatelji su riječi koje izgledaju ili zvuče slično riječima u vašem jeziku, ali imaju drugačija značenja, npr.:
        ○ data (podaci)
        ○ date (datum): Želio bih odrediti datum.
        ○ realise (shvatiti): Shvaćaš li koliko je opasno?

● Pisanje – struktura pripovijetke
===================================================
    ○ Pregled
    ○ Kratke priče se obično pišu iz perspektive prvog lica (glavni lik priča priču) ili trećeg lica (pisac priča priču, ali nije lik u njoj).
    ○ Početak
        ● Privucite pozornost čitatelja dramatičnim ili tajanstvenim uvodom, izjavom jednog od glavnih likova ili detaljem koji se tiče važnog mjesta ili osobe iz priče.
        ● Postavite scenu. Recite gdje i kada se priča odvija i predstavite glavne likove.
    ○ Sredina
        ● Koristite pripovjedna vremena za opisivanje radnje.
        ● Dodajte dramatičnost i zanimljivost kraćim rečenicama.
        ● Koristite jezik koji se obraća osjetilima kako biste opisali što likovi vide, čuju i osjećaju.
        ● Izvještavajte govor koristeći razne glagole.
        ● Dajte likovima glas koristeći direktni govor.
    ○ Kraj
        ● Zaključite priču obratom, naučenom lekcijom, donesenom odlukom ili kasnijim događajem povezanim s pričom.
