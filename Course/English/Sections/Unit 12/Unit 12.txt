
● Gramatika

    ● Budući oblici (PREGLED)
        ○ Present simple
            ○ koristi se za govor o fiksnom budućem događaju, prema rasporedu, programu
        ○ Present continuous
            ○ koristi se za govor o budućem dogovoru (često se spominje vrijeme, datum ili mjesto)
            ○ They're giving prizes to the winners of the cookery competition tomorrow in the Town hall
        ○ Future simple (will)
            ○ koristi se za spontanu odluku donesenu u trenutku govora – reakcija na okolnosti
                ○ Wait for me. I'll help you peel the carrots
            ○ koristi se u pitanjima s I i we, za ponude i prijedloge – tada se koristi shall (ne will)
                ○ These bags look heavy, shall I carry them for you?
            ○ koristi se za govor o budućem predviđanju – na temelju mišljenja, predviđanja ili iskustva
                ○ I can lend you this cookery book but I'll probably need it next week
        ○ Be going to + infinitiv
            ○ koristi se za govor o budućoj namjeri (nešto što ste već odlučili učiniti)
                ○ What are you going to prepare for dinner?
            ○ koristi se za govor o budućem predviđanju (na temelju onoga što vidite ili znate)
                ○ This cake looks really sweet. Sue isn't going to have any of it

    ● Future Continuous i Future Perfect (PREGLED)
        ○ Future Continuous
            ○ koristi se za govor o duljim nezavršenim radnjama koje će biti u tijeku u određeno vrijeme u budućnosti
                ○ We will be making a cake at 5 p.m.
            ○ tvorba
                ○ Potvrdni oblik
                    ○ I/You/He/She/It/We/They + 'll + be working at 10 a.m.
                ○ Niječni oblik
                    ○ I/You/He/She/It/We/They + won't + be working at 10 a.m.
                ○ Da/Ne pitanja
                    ○ Will I/you/he/she/it/we/they be working at 10 a.m.?
                ○ Kratki odgovori
                    ○ Yes, I/you/he/she/it/we/they will.
                    ○ No, I/you/he/she/it/we/they won't.
                ○ Pitanja s uptitnim riječima
                    ○ What will I/you/he/she/it/we/they be doing at 10 a.m.?
                ○ Pitanja na subjekt
                    ○ Who will be working at 10 a.m.?
            ○ (SLIKA 1)
        ○ Future Perfect
            ○ koristi se za govor o radnji koja će biti završena prije određenog trenutka u budućnosti
                ○ I will have cooked all the dishes by 3 o’clock.
            ○ tvorba
                ○ Potvrdni oblik
                    ○ I/You/He/She/It/We/They + 'll (will) have finished lunch by 2 p.m.
                ○ Niječni oblik
                    ○ I/You/He/She/It/We/They + won't (will not) have finished lunch by 2 p.m.
                ○ Da/Ne pitanja
                    ○ Will I/you/he/she/it/we/they have finished lunch by 2 p.m.?
                ○ Kratki odgovori
                    ○ Yes, I/you/he/she/it/we/they will.
                    ○ No, I/you/he/she/it/we/they won't.
                ○ Pitanja s uptitnim riječima
                    ○ What will I/you/he/she/it/we/they have finished lunch by 2 p.m.?
                ○ Pitanja na subjekt
                    ○ Who will have finished lunch by 2 p.m.?
            ○ (SLIKA 2)

● Jezik

    ● Question tags (kratka pitanja na kraju rečenice)
        ○ dodaju se radi potvrde ili postavljanja pitanja
        ○ tvore se pomoćnim glagolom (be, have, do) ili modalnim glagolom (can, will, should) + zamjenica (she)
        ○ nakon potvrdne izjave koristi se niječni tag
            ○ You're going to use stale bread, aren't you?
        ○ nakon niječne izjave koristi se potvrdni tag
            ○ You haven't eaten unripe bananas, have you?
        ○ Posebni slučajevi
            ○ u rečenici s I'm, question tag je aren't I
                ○ I am a good cook, aren't I?
            ○ u imperativima, question tag je will you
                ○ Don't order too much food, will you?
                ○ Grate some Parmesan cheese, will you?
            ○ u rečenicama s let's, question tag je shall we
                ○ Let's have some pizza, shall we?
                ○ Let's have a quick snack, shall we?
            ○ u rečenici sa zamjenicom that kao subjektom, u tagu koristimo it
                ○ That's pumpkin soup, isn't it?
        ○ Question tags za potvrdu – silazna intonacija
            ○ You know how to cook scrambled eggs, don't you? (gotovo smo sigurni da osoba to zna)
        ○ Question tags za postavljanje pitanja – uzlazna intonacija
            ○ Your sister is a vegan, isn't she? (nismo sigurni je li osoba vegan)
        ○ Ponovno: tvorba – pomoćni glagol (be, have, do) ili modalni glagol (can, will, should) + zamjenica (I, you, she)
        ○ Nakon potvrdne izjave → niječni tag
            ○ You're going to use stale bread, aren't you?
        ○ Nakon niječne izjave → potvrdni tag
            ○ You haven't eaten unripe bananas, have you?
        ○ Posebni slučajevi (ponovno)
            ○ I'm a good cook, aren't I?
            ○ Grate some Parmesan cheese, will you?
            ○ Don't order too much food, will you?
            ○ Let's have a quick snack, shall we?
            ○ That's pumpkin soup, isn't it?

    ● Neizravna pitanja (PREGLED)
        ○ koriste se da bi bila uljudnija
        ○ red riječi je isti kao u potvrdnim izjavama
            ○ Could you tell me what the dates and the times of the next course are?
            ○ I would also like to know whether I need to bring anything else.
        ○ za da/ne pitanja koristi se if/whether (ne koristi se do/does/did)
        ○ Izravno: (1)
        ○ Neizravno: Could you tell me what the dates and the times of the next course are?
        ○ Izravno: (2)
        ○ Neizravno: I would also like to know whether I need to bring anything else.
        ○ Napomena: Red riječi u neizravnim pitanjima isti je kao u potvrdnim izjavama.
        ○ Za da/ne pitanja koristi se if/whether.
        ○ Ne koristi se do, does ili did.

● Pisanje

    ● Formalni e-mail s traženjem informacija i pojašnjenja (PREGLED)
        ○ Započnite e-mail uljudno
            ○ Dear Sir or Dear Madam
            ○ Dear Sir/Madam (ako je spol nepoznat)
            ○ Dear Mr, Dear Mrs ili Dear Miss (za mladu ženu) + prezime
            ○ Dear Ms + prezime (za neudanu ženu ili ako niste sigurni)
        ○ Ne koristite kratice: ne Thanks, već Thank you
        ○ Neformalni izrazi: ne lots of, već several; a number of
        ○ Bez kontrakcija: ne I'm, već I am
        ○ U prvom odlomku uputite na pismo/e-mail/oglas na koji odgovarate i navedite zašto pišete
            ○ Thank you for your letter/email informing me that.../regarding ...
        ○ Ako nešto nije jasno, zatražite pojašnjenje
            ○ Could you confirm/explain when/where/what/how/whether/if ...?
        ○ U završnom odlomku spomenite da biste željeli odgovor
            ○ I look forward to receiving your reply soon.
        ○ Završite e-mail uljudno
            ○ Yours sincerely (ako znate ime osobe kojoj pišete)
            ○ Yours faithfully (ako ste pismo započeli s Dear Sir/Madam)

● Govor

    ● U restoranu (PREGLED)
        ○ Naručivanje hrane
            ○ Can I order, please?
            ○ Do you have any vegetarian dishes?
            ○ Can I have chips with that?
            ○ Could I have the bill, please?
        ○ Traženje informacija neizravnim pitanjima
            ○ Can you tell me what the soup is?
            ○ Can you tell me what this meal is?
            ○ Do you know what sauce is?
            ○ I'd like to know if there is vinegar in it.
