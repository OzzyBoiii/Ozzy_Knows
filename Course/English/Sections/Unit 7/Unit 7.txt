                The Passive (OVERVIEWED)
                - used either when it is not important or you do not know who performed the action (instead of a person, we use the word by)
                - "This shopping mall is visited by around 50,000 people every day
                - they have the same tenses as active verbs
                - form: be + Past Participle
                - (+) Fairtrade products are grown in developing countries
                - (-) Fairtrade products are not grown in developed countries
                - (?) Where are Fairtrade products grown?
                - usage in Present Simple
                - active ("People do not pay farmers enough")
                - passive ("Farmers are not payed enough")
                - usage in Past Simple
                - active ("In 1997, somebody formed one International Fairtrade organisation")
                - passive (in 1997, one International Fairtrade organisation was formed")
                - usage in Present Perfect
                - active ("Fairtrade has improved the lives of seven million people in developing countries")
                - passive ("The lives of seven milion people in developing countries have improved by Fairtrade")
                - usage with Modal verbs
                - active ("We need more improved conditions.")
                - passive ("Conditions must be improved.")

Language
<p>
                <!---->
                Indefinite pronouns <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                - used to talk about non-specific people, objects or places
                - they are all used with singular verb forms
                - "Everybody is fashionable in their own way."
                - Pronouns list
                - someone/somebody, something, somewhere
                - used in affirmative sentences and requests/offers
                - "Somebody has eft their receipt on the counter."
                - anybody/anyone, anything, anywhere
                - used in negative sentences and questions
                - "I have not bought anything made of leather for a long time."
                - no one/nobody, nothing, nowhere
                - used in affirmative sentences with a negative meaning
                - "There will be nowhere to park."
                - none (of)
                - used when we talk about three or more objects or people
                - nouns are not used after none
                - "I was looking for milk but I found none."
              </p>

<p>

                A formal written complaint <br>(OVERVIEWED)
                - Open and close the email or letter formally
                - Give a reason for writting
                - Say what you bought and when
                - Explain the problem giving details
                - Tell the reader what you expect them to do
                - Use formal language with no contractions

Speaking
<p>
                <!---->
                Shopping for clothes <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                - Shop assistant
                - Can I help you?
                - Would you like to try it on.
                - The changing rooms are over there.
                - How would you like to pay?
                - Make sure you keep your receipt.
                - Customer
                - Excuse me, I am looking for a top.
                - I am a size 10
                - Do you have this in a size 12, please?    
                - By Cash/credit card please.
                - If it does not work, can we have a refund?     
                Making complaints
                - Shop assistant
                - What is wrong with it
                - Do you have your previous purchased item?
                - We can exchange it for a new one.
                - Customer
                - I bought this dress last week but the zip does not work
                - I think it is faulty./It shrank./There is a hole in it./The colour ran
                - I would like a refund please.
              </p>
