                - introduced with these examples 
                - if ("If Mark does not hand in his homework today, his teacher will get really angry.") 
                - unless ("Amy will not make any progress unless she works systematically.") 
                - Time clauses 
                - introduced with these examples 
                - when ("When the new term starts, the teachers will meet up.") 
                - before ("Before i take Chemistry and Physics for my A levels, I will talk to my teachers.") 
                - after ("After you mark your homework, you will see your mistakes.") 
                - as soon as ("The kids will feel better as soon as the school breaks up for holiday.") 
                - Present Simple is used in future times and conditional clauses 
                - formation: Subordinate clause (if/unless/when/before/after/as soon as + Present Simple) + Main clause (will (not) + verb) 
                - "My sister will never succeed unless she learns from her mistakes." 
                - Either clause may come first in the sentence 
                - We put a comma at the end of the subordinate clause if it comes first in the sentence 
                - "As soon as I get my new timetable, I will let you know about the time of our meeting." 
                - We do not put a comma at the end of the mainclause if it comes first in the sentence 
                - "I will let you know about the time of our meeting as soon as I get my new timetable."
