                REPORTED SPEECH - STATEMENTS <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                When we want to report what someone said, we can use Direct or Reported Speech.
                In Direct Speech the speaker’s words are not changed in any way:
                ‘Your son draws well.’ ➞ They said, ‘Your son draws well.’
                In Reported Speech we often report the speaker’s words using verbs like add, claim, explain, point out, reply, say, suggest, tell and the pronoun that (which can be omitted). The verb tell takes an object (me, us, John, my friend, etc.).
                ‘I sell paintings.’  ➞ She told me/said (that) she sold paintings.
                We make the following changes in Reported Speech:

                Reported Speech – questions and imperatives <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                Questions
                To report questions, we use the verb ask and make the same changes as in reported statements (tenses, pronouns, time expressions, words referring to places, etc.) 
                When we report yes/no questions, we use if or whether:
                ‘Has Darina answered all the questions?’ ➞ Jan asked me if/whether Darina had answered all the questions.
                ‘Do you often watch TV?’ ➞ I asked her if/whether she often watched TV. 
                When we report wh- questions, we keep the question word (e.g. what, who, how, where, when):
                ‘How old are you?’ ➞ The policeman asked the boy how old he was.
                ‘When did John and Mary split up?’ ➞ Sam asked us when John and Mary had split up. 
                Imperatives
                To report imperatives, we use the verbs ask or tell, an object (noun or pronoun) and (not) to + infinitive: 
                ‘Please stop talking!’ ➞ The director asked us to stop talking. 
                ‘Don’t take any photos of me!’ ➞ Darina told the reporter not to take any photos of her.

Language
<p>
                <!---->
                Nouns <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                Countable nouns have a singular and a plural form:
                a review – reviews 
                The plural of nouns:
                is formed by adding the following endings:
                -s 
                a play – plays, a vocalist – vocalists 
                -ies, when the noun ends with a consonant + -y:
                a story – stories, a comedy – comedies 
                -es, when the noun ends with -s, -ss, -x, -ch, -sh:
                a watch – watches, a sketch – sketches 
                -ves, when the noun ends with -fe, -f:
                a shelf – shelves, life – lives 
                may be the same as the singular form:
                a species – species, a series – series, a fish – fish 
                may differ from the singular form:
                a man – men, a tooth – teeth
                Uncountable nouns 
                only have a singular form:
                Knowledge is the key to the door of happiness.
                Don’t you think that your hair is a bit too short?
                in sentences, you only use a singular verb form:
                The lead guitarist’s luggage hasn’t arrived yet so we can’t start the rehearsals.
                are used with such expressions of quantity as: some, much, a piece of:
                Last night there was much traffic in the city centre because of a light show.
                end with -s and although they look like plural nouns, they are uncountable and singular: news, physics, politics, statistics.
                You can’t put a/an or a number in front of uncountable nouns:
                Tom gave me two pieces of contradictory information about the concert. (NOT Tom gave me two contradictory informations.)
                Plural nouns 
                only have a plural form and you can’t put a/an or a number in front of them:
                Where exactly are the police headquarters?
                some of them consist of two parts (e.g. clothes) and are used with a pair of:
                I bought a pair of trousers and a jacket yesterday.

                Modifiers with base and extreme adjectives <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                
                You use different adverbs to modify base or extreme adjectives. 
                Base adjectives are gradable e.g. very bad is worse than quite bad.
                 Extreme adjectives are non-gradable e.g. impossible means impossible – you can’t be ‘more’ impossible or ‘less’ impossible.

Writting
<p>
                <!---->
                An article reviewing an event <br> (OVERVIEWED)
                Begin with an interesting, funny or unusual title to attract the reader’s attention.
                Hold the reader’s attention with an introduction which asks a question or gives interesting facts.
                Describe the event using a variety of adjectives and modifiers.
                Make your descriptions interesting by describing what you saw, heard, smelled and tasted.
                Include personal opinions and suggestions.
                Finish with a recommendation for the reader.

● REPORTED SPEECH – PITANJA I ZAPOVIJEDI  
○ (Pregled)  
    ○ Pitanja:  
        ● Za prenošenje pitanja koristimo glagol "pitati" (ask) i činimo iste promjene kao u neizravnim izjavama (vremena, zamjenice, vremenski izrazi, riječi za mjesta, itd.).  
        ● Kada prenosimo da/ne pitanja, koristimo "ako" ili "da li":  
            ○ ‘Je li Darina odgovorila na sva pitanja?’  
                ● ➞ Jan me pitao je li Darina odgovorila na sva pitanja.  
            ○ ‘Gledaš li često TV?’  
                ● ➞ Pitao sam je gleda li često TV.  
        ● Kada prenosimo upitne riječi (wh- pitanja), zadržavamo upitnu riječ (npr. što, tko, kako, gdje, kada):  
            ○ ‘Koliko imaš godina?’  
                ● ➞ Policajac je pitao dječaka koliko ima godina.  
            ○ ‘Kada su se Ivan i Marija razišli?’  
                ● ➞ Sam nas je pitao kada su se Ivan i Marija razišli.  
    ○ Zapovijedi:  
        ● Za prenošenje zapovijedi koristimo glagole "zamoliti" (ask) ili "reći" (tell), objekt (imenicu ili zamjenicu) i (ne) + infinitiv:  
            ○ ‘Molim vas, prestanite pričati!’  
                ● ➞ Redatelj nas je zamolio da prestanemo pričati.  
            ○ ‘Nemoj me slikati!’  
                ● ➞ Darina je rekla reporteru da je ne slika.

● IMENICE  
○ (Pregled)  
    ○ Brojive imenice:  
        ● Imaju jedninu i množinu: recenzija – recenzije  
        ● Množina imenica tvori se dodavanjem sljedećih nastavaka:  
            ○ -s: predstava – predstave, vokal – vokali  
            ○ -ies, kada imenica završava na suglasnik + -y: priča – priče, komedija – komedije  
            ○ -es, kada imenica završava na -s, -ss, -x, -ch, -sh: sat – satovi, skica – skice  
            ○ -ves, kada imenica završava na -fe, -f: polica – police, život – životi  
            ○ mogu biti ista kao oblik za jedninu: vrsta – vrste, serija – serije, riba – ribe  
            ○ mogu se razlikovati od oblika za jedninu: čovjek – ljudi, zub – zubi  
    ○ Nebrojive imenice:  
        ● Imaju samo oblik za jedninu:  
            ○ Znanje je ključ do vrata sreće.  
            ○ Zar ne misliš da ti je kosa malo prekratka?  
        ● U rečenicama se koriste samo s glagolom u jednini:  
            ○ Prtljaga glavnog gitarista još nije stigla pa ne možemo početi probe.  
        ● Koriste se s izrazima za količinu kao što su:  
            ○ nešto, mnogo, komad:  
                ● Sinoć je bilo mnogo prometa u centru grada zbog svjetlosne predstave.  
        ● Završavaju na -s i iako izgledaju kao množina, one su nebrojive i jedninske:  
            ○ vijesti, fizika, politika, statistika.  
        ● Ne možemo staviti član "a/an" ili broj ispred nebrojivih imenica:  
            ○ Tom mi je dao dva komada kontradiktornih informacija o koncertu. (NE Tom mi je dao dvije kontradiktorne informacije.)  
    ○ Množinske imenice:  
        ● Imaju samo oblik za množinu i ne možemo staviti "a/an" ili broj ispred njih:  
            ○ Gdje se točno nalazi sjedište policije?  
        ● Neke se sastoje od dva dijela (npr. odjeća) i koriste se s "par":  
            ○ Kupio sam par hlača i jaknu jučer.
