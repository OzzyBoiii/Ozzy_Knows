● Past Perfect  
   ○ Koristi se da se naglasi kako se jedna radnja u prošlosti dogodila prije druge prošle radnje  
   ○ Tvori se = had + glagol u trećem obliku (Past Participle)  
   ○ Tvorba rečenice = radnja prije prošle radnje (his heart had stopped) + prošla radnja koja se dogodila nakon prethodne (By the time they got to him)  
   ○ Primjer = "By the time they got to him, his heart had stopped."  
   ○ Tvorba  
      ● Potvrdni oblik = I/he/she/it/you/we/they + had + watched TV.  
      ● Niječni oblik = I/he/she/it/you/we/they + had not + watched TV.  
      ● Da/Ne pitanja = Had + I/he/she/it + watching TV?  
         ○ Odgovor = Yes, I/he/she/it/you/we/they had / No, I/he/she/it/you/we/they had not  
      ● Pitanja s upitnim riječima = What + had + I/he/she/it/you/we/they + watched?  
      ● Pitanja na subjekt = Who + had + watched TV?  
   (SLIKA 1)

● Reported Speech  
   ○ Koristi se za prenošenje tuđeg govora  
   ○ Razlikujemo direktni govor (doslovno citiranje) i indirektni ili upravni govor (prenošenje izjave)  
   ○ Glagolska vremena i zamjenice se mijenjaju ovisno o kontekstu  
   ○ Često koristimo glagole say (that), tell somebody (that) i indirektne objekte (me, us, itd.)  
   ○ Primjeri  
      ● "She told me/said (that) the match was great."  
      ● "He told me that he had felt dizzy."  
   ○ Promjene glagolskih vremena

● Phrasal verbs (frazeološki glagoli)  
   ○ Tvore se od glavnog glagola i prijedloga ili priloga  
      ● look (main verb) + for (preposition) = look for  
   ○ Vrste phrasal verbs  
      ● Bez objekta (neprijelazni) = join in, work out, fit in, pass out  
         ○ Primjer = "Yesterday was very hot and a few people passed out because of that."  
      ● S objektom koji dolazi iza glagola ili između glavnog glagola i prijedloga  
         ○ Zamjenice (him, them, it) moraju doći isključivo između glavnog glagola i prijedloga  
         ○ Primjeri  
            ● "I took up handball and rugby."  
            ● "I took handball and rugby up at the same time."  
            ● "I took them up at the same time."  
      ● S objektom (i imenica i zamjenica mogu doći iza prijedloga)  
         ○ Primjer = "Every spring my brother suffers from an allergy."  
      ● Mogu biti i trodijelni te zahtijevaju objekt iza sebe  
         ○ Primjer = "We look forward to playing basketball in our new court."  

● Strukture s glagolom make  
   ○ Glagol make uvijek zahtijeva objekt, a nakon njega dolazi ili infinitiv bez "to", ili pridjev, ili imenica  
   ○ make + objekt + infinitiv bez "to"  
      ● Primjer = "Make time endless."  
   ○ make + objekt + pridjev/imenica  
      ● Primjer = "Make turtles non-endangered."

● Reader's comment (Komentar čitatelja)  
   ○ Poveznice (linkers) koje se koriste u odgovoru na komentar  
      ● Za izražavanje mišljenja = I think / Personally, I do not think  
      ● Za dodavanje daljnjih točaka = In fact ... / I also agree that ... / I also think that ... / What is more ...  
      ● Za izražavanje suprotnog mišljenja = On the other hand, I strongly disagree ... / However, I believe ...  
      ● Za zaključivanje = Therefore ... / So ... / That is why ... / For this reason ...

● Appointment to doctor (Dogovaranje pregleda kod liječnika)  
   ○ Dijagnoza  
      ● When did the pain start?  
      ● I am going to examine you / take your temperature.  
      ● I am going to order a blood test etc.  
      ● Breathe in and out  
      ● Spread your arms wide  
      ● Lie down, please  
      ● If I press here, does it hurt?  
      ● I think you have got indigestion / an infection / a virus etc.  
      ● You are probably allergic to etc.  
   ○ Liječenje  
      ● You should eat more slowly / go on a diet  
      ● You need to drink more water  
      ● I am going to write you a prescription  
      ● I am going to make an appointment for you  
      ● Swallow one tablet after each meal
