● Grammar

○ Present tenses - question forms (OVERVIEWED)
○ to make questions, put an auxiliary (do, be, have) before the subject of the main verb

(TABLICA 1)
Present Simple
Yes/No questions
Do + I/you/we/they + speak English?
Does + he/she/it + speak English?
Wh- questions
What languages + do + I/you/we/they + speak?
What languages + does + he/she/it + speak?
Subject questions
Who + speaks + English?

(TABLICA 2)
Present Continuous
Yes/No questions
Am + I + working now?
Are + you/we/they + working now?
Is + he/she/it + working now?
Wh- questions
What + am + I + doing?
What + is + he/she/it + doing?
What + are + you/we/they + doing?
Subject questions
Who + is working + now?

(SLIKA 1)

○ Present Perfect
○ Yes/No questions
● Have + I/you/we/they + swum in a river?
● Has + he/she/it + swum in a river?
○ Wh- questions
● What + have + I/you/we/they + done?
● What + has + he/she/it + done?
○ Subject questions
● Who + has swum + in a river?
○ when you ask about the subject, you do not use the Present Simple auxiliary do/does
● Who inspires you?

● Verb + -ing or verb + to + infinitive (OVERVIEWED)

○ verb + -ing
● used with following verbs
○ verbs expressing emotions (enjoy, hate, like, love)
● "I hate wearing a suit and a tie."
○ particular verbs and verb phrases (avoid, can't stand, consider, spend time, prefer)
● "Laura prefers texting to sending emails."

○ verb + to + infinitive
● used with following verbs
○ most verbs expressing plans, decisions, intensions and willingness (hope, want, decide, choose)
● "Tim wants to study Law."
○ particular verbs (agree, cannot afford, manage, need, pretend, refuse)
● "Why do you refuse to lend me your shoes?"
○ verbs expressing preferences (would like, would love, would prefer)
● "I would like to stay at home today."

● Language

○ So and such (OVERVIEWED)
○ either used when we want to stress the noun they precede or when we want to stress an adjective or noun and focus on the consequences of events described in the main sentence clause
● "My younger sister has got so many toys that my parents want to give some away."

○ pronoun so
● formation = so + adjective
● It is used before
○ adjectives without nouns
● "My aunt Tanya is so inspiring."
○ quantifying expressions (many/much) followed by a noun
● "So many friends are coming to the party."

○ pronoun such
● formation = such + noun phrase
● It is used before
○ adjectives followed by nouns
○ the following nouns:
● "Such people will always help you in need."
● "Angela always has such luck."
● Information about it
○ the indefinite article a/an is used if it precedes a countable singular noun
● "Uncle Tom has such an interesting life."
○ we do not use any article if it precedes a plural countable noun
● "Every morning Josh goes for such long walks with his dogs."

● Adjective + preposition (OVERVIEWED)
○ used to give information about yourself
● I am crazy/excited/mad/passionate/serious/worried about
● I am bad/good/useless at
● I am involved in
● I am afraid of
● I am keen on
● I am disappointed/obsessed with
○ Questions can end in a preposition
● What subject are you good at?

● Writing

○ A personal email/letter (OVERVIEWED)
● start writing with a friendly greeting (Dear Nick,/Hi Jo)
● do not use full forms, use contractions (you're (not you are)/I'm)
● occasionally use emotions (💀) or abbreviations (Bye 4 now)
● ask questions to show you want a reply (What about you?)
● finish writing with a friendly goodbye (All the best/Bye 4 now)
