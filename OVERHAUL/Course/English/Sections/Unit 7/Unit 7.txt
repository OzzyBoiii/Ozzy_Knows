
● The Passive (OVERVIEWED)
	○ koristi se kada nije važno ili ne znamo tko je izvršio radnju
	○ umjesto osobe, koristimo riječ "by"
		● This shopping mall is visited by around 50,000 people every day
	○ imaju ista vremena kao i aktivni glagoli
	○ tvore se: ● be + Past Participle
		● (+) Fairtrade products are grown in developing countries
		○ (-) Fairtrade products are not grown in developed countries
		○ (?) Where are Fairtrade products grown?
	○ uporaba u Present Simple
		○ aktiv: "People do not pay farmers enough"
		○ pasiv: "Farmers are not paid enough"
	○ uporaba u Past Simple
		○ aktiv: "In 1997, somebody formed one International Fairtrade organisation"
		○ pasiv: "In 1997, one International Fairtrade organisation was formed"
	○ uporaba u Present Perfect
		○ aktiv: "Fairtrade has improved the lives of seven million people in developing countries"
		○ pasiv: "The lives of seven million people in developing countries have been improved by Fairtrade"
	○ uporaba s modalnim glagolima
		○ aktiv: "We need improved conditions."
		○ pasiv: "Conditions must be improved."

● Quantifiers (OVERVIEWED)
	○ različiti izrazi se koriste za govor o količini
(TABLICA 1)
	● Countable nouns
		○ How many? ("How many friends have you got?")
		○ (very) few ("I have got (very) few friends.")
		○ a few ("I have got a few friends.")
		○ some ("I have got some friends.")
		○ many ("I have got many friends.")
		○ a lot of/lots of ("I have got a lot of/lots of friends.")
		○ too many ("I have got too many friends.")
		○ any ("I have not got any friends.")
	○ Uncountable nouns
		○ How much? ("How much money have you got?")
		○ (very) little ("I have got (very) little money.")
		○ a little ("I have got a little money.")
		○ much ("I have not got much money.")
		○ too much ("I have got too much money.")

● Indefinite pronouns (OVERVIEWED)
	○ koriste se za govor o nespecifičnim osobama, predmetima ili mjestima
	○ svi se koriste s ● glagolima u jednini
		● "Everybody is fashionable in their own way."
	○ lista zamjenica
		● someone/somebody, something, somewhere
			○ koriste se u potvrdnim rečenicama i zahtjevima/ponudama
				● "Somebody has left their receipt on the counter."
		● anybody/anyone, anything, anywhere
			○ koriste se u odričnim rečenicama i pitanjima
				● "I have not bought anything made of leather for a long time."
		● no one/nobody, nothing, nowhere
			○ koriste se u potvrdnim rečenicama s odričnim značenjem
				● "There will be nowhere to park."
		● none (of)
			○ koristi se kada govorimo o tri ili više predmeta ili osoba
			○ imenice se ne koriste iza "none"
				● "I was looking for milk but I found none."
(TABLICA 2)
	○ People
		● someone/somebody
		● anyone/anybody
		● no one/nobody
		● everyone/everybody
	○ Things
		● something
		● anything
		● nothing
		● everything
	○ Places
		● somewhere
		● anywhere
		● nowhere
		● everywhere
	○ Modifying indefinite pronouns
		● to infinitive
			○ "There is nobody to go shopping with me."
		● an adjective
			○ "Joan needs to go somewhere quiet to rest."
		● a relative pronoun (who, which, that)
			○ "A debtor is someone who has spent more money than he has got."

	○ jezik se može učiniti formalnijim izbjegavanjem kontrakcija i korištenjem formalnijih riječi i izraza
(TABLICA 3)

		● I sent them back
		● You swapped them
		● I got another pair
		● I'm so angry
		● I want all my money back

		● I want my refund

● Shopping for clothes (OVERVIEWED)
	○ Shop assistant
		● Can I help you?
		● Would you like to try it on?
		● The changing rooms are over there.
		● How would you like to pay?
		● Make sure you keep your receipt.
	○ Customer
		● Excuse me, I am looking for a top.
		● I am a size 10.
		● Do you have this in a size 12, please?
		● By cash/credit card please.
		● If it does not work, can we have a refund?

● Making complaints
	○ Shop assistant
		● What is wrong with it?
		● Do you have your previously purchased item?
		● We can exchange it for a new one.
	○ Customer
		● I bought this dress last week but the zip does not work.
		● I think it is faulty. / It shrank. / There is a hole in it. / The colour ran.
		● I would like a refund please.
