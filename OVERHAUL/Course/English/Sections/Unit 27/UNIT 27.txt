Grammar

===================================================

Phrasal verbs

●	Phrasal verbs su značenjske cjeline sastavljene od glagola i čestice (obično priloga).

●	Phrasal verb je kombinacija koja poprima novo značenje, neovisno o izvornom značenju glagola.

○	We ran out of the house. (značenje glagola se ne mijenja, a out of se odnosi na kuću)

○	We ran into Mark on Saturday. (značenje se promijenilo ➝ run + into znači 'sresti')

●	Phrasal verbs se često koriste u engleskom jeziku.

●	Obično se koriste u manje formalnim kontekstima od jednorječnih glagola.

○	We need to sort out the problem. (manje formalno)

○	We need to resolve the problem. (formalnije)

●	Phrasal verbs nemaju uvijek jednorječni ekvivalent, ali kad ga imaju, u formalnom engleskom biramo jednorječni glagol, a u neutralnom i neformalnom kontekstu phrasal verb.

Transitive and intransitive phrasal verbs

●	Phrasal verbs mogu biti prijelazni (upotrebljavaju se s objektom) ili neprijelazni (bez objekta).

○	It’s cold, put your jacket on. (your jacket = objekt, put on je prijelazni phrasal verb)

○	The policeman let him off with a fine. (him = objekt, let off je prijelazni phrasal verb)

○	The realisation of the disaster slowly began to sink in. (nema objekta, sink in je neprijelazni phrasal verb)

○	I’m sure the price of some vegetables will go down. (nema objekta, go down je neprijelazni phrasal verb)

●	Neki phrasal verbs mogu biti i prijelazni i neprijelazni.

○	Cheer up! I’m sure everything will be all right in the end. (neprijelazni)

○	My best friend always knows how to cheer me up when I am upset. (prijelazni)

●	Ponekad se isti glagol može upotrebljavati i prijelazno i neprijelazno, ali s razlikom u značenju.

○	It took years to pay off the loan. (= vratiti novac, prijelazni)

○	In the end, eating carefully and avoiding fatty foods paid off. (= dati dobre rezultate, neprijelazni)

●	Prijelazni phrasal verbs mogu biti razdvojivi ili nerazdvojivi.

●	Kod razdvojivih phrasal verbs, čestica se može odvojiti od glagola: objekt može doći između glagola i čestice, ili iza čestice.

○	The nutritionist said I should give bread up. (objekt bread razdvaja glagol i česticu)

○	The nutritionist said I should give up bread. (objekt bread dolazi iza čestice up)

●	Kada je objekt zamjenica, uvijek dolazi između glagola i čestice.

○	The nutritionist said I should give it up. (objekt it je zamjenica, dolazi između glagola i čestice)

●	Prilog ili pasivni objekt ne stavljamo između glagola i čestice.

○	She backed up their ideas forcefully. (NE She backed forcefully up their ideas.)

○	The plan for the food festival was put forward by the promoters. (NE The plan for the food festival was put by the promoters forward.)

●	Primjeri prijelaznih razdvojivih glagola: let down, lay off, back up, bring up, pay off, blow up, call off, turn on, turn off.

●	Kod nerazdvojivih phrasal verbs, glagol i čestica se nikada ne odvajaju: objekt uvijek dolazi iza čestice.

○	As delicious as pancakes are, you can’t live on pancakes alone. (objekt pancakes dolazi iza čestice)

○	As delicious as pancakes are, you can’t live on them alone. (objekt them dolazi iza čestice)

●	Primjeri prijelaznih nerazdvojivih glagola: look for, look into, break into, call for, fall for, get over, go through.

Three-word phrasal verbs

●	Trorječni phrasal verbs sastoje se od glagola + priložne čestice + prijedloga, a poznati su i kao phrasal-prepositional verbs.

●	Prijelazni su i nerazdvojivi. Objekt uvijek slijedi završni prijedlog.

○	He put up with the pain for as long as he could but finally asked for a painkiller. (objekt the pain dolazi iza završnog prijedloga with)

●	Modifikacijski prilog može se staviti samo između priložne čestice i prijedloga.

○	He put up bravely with the pain for as long as he could, but finally asked for a painkiller. ILI He put up with the pain bravely for as long as he could, but finally asked for a painkiller.

●	Uobičajeni primjeri trorječnih phrasal verbs: back out of, catch up on, catch up with, drop in on, face up to, get down to, look down on, make away with, stand up for.

Language

===================================================

Particles in phrasal verbs

●	Budući da phrasal verbs poprimaju značenje često potpuno nepovezano s izvornim glagolom, teško je pronaći značenje phrasal verb-a gledajući glagol.

●	Značenje se obično mora izvući iz konteksta.

●	Vrijedno je napomenuti da isti phrasal verb može imati potpuno različito značenje ovisno o kontekstu u kojem se pojavljuje.

○	The fire was put out quickly. (= ugašen)

○	He looked very put out when I suggested a change of plan. (= iznerviran)

●	Ipak, čestica ponekad može pomoći (ali ne uvijek) u indiciranju općeg značenja phrasal verb-a.

○	across: from one point to another
●	get / put across = komunicirati
●	come across = pronaći, slučajno naići

○	apart: separate
●	take apart = rastaviti na dijelove
●	set apart = staviti dalje, odvojiti

○	aside: to one side for future use
●	set aside = uštedjeti (novac, vrijeme) za posebnu namjenu
●	put aside = odložiti, spremiti
●	move to another point / somewhere else
●	give away = otkriti, pokloniti
●	put away = pospremiti, staviti na pravo mjesto, pojesti
●	take away = odnijeti s jednog mjesta na drugo

○	back: move to a previous point
●	take back = povući
●	bring back = vratiti
●	cut back = smanjiti na nižu / prethodnu razinu

○	down: not working as it should / moving to a lower point / older to younger
●	break down = stroj ne radi
●	cut down = smanjiti
●	hand down = prenijeti sa starije na mlađu osobu
●	let down = razočarati nečinjenjem nečega što ste trebali

○	forward: move to the next point / a point ahead
●	bring forward = pomaknuti na raniji datum / predstaviti (npr. plan)
●	put forward = predložiti

○	in: within / inside / from an outside point towards the centre
●	bring in = zaraditi
●	hand in = predati / vratiti
●	take in = apsorbirati, razumjeti, smanjiti, pružiti sklonište

○	off: separation / separating into parts / leaving / stopping / moving out from a point
●	cut off = odvojiti
●	set off = krenuti na put
●	turn off = isključiti nešto
●	give off = ispuštati

○	on: progressing / continuing / covering / performing / operating
●	come on = napredovati
●	carry / go on = nastaviti
●	put on = odjenuti, izvesti (predstavu), uključiti
●	take on = prihvatiti odgovornost

○	out: moving towards the outside / removing / bringing to a conclusion / giving something to people / extending
●	cut out = prestati koristiti
●	carry out = učiniti i dovršiti
●	bring / come out = postati dostupno
●	put out = smetati / iznervirati

○	over: down / from one position across to another
●	knock over = srušiti
●	take over = preuzeti odgovornost

○	through: move from one point to another / complete
●	put through = uspješno završiti proces
●	think through = razmisliti o svim aspektima

○	round: from the start back to the original position
●	bring round = uvjeriti, pomoći nekome da se osvijesti
●	hand round = dati svima / podijeliti

○	together: join
●	put together = sastaviti nešto u jednu cjelinu
●	get together = sastati se, okupiti se

○	up: completing / introducing / increasing / moving towards
●	break up = raskinuti, završiti
●	bring up = uvesti novu temu
●	go up = povećati se

●	Neki phrasal verbs mogu se pretvoriti u imenice. Obično se tvore spajanjem glagola i čestice.
○	break down ➝ breakdown
○	break in ➝ break–in
○	turn out ➝ turnout

●	U nekim slučajevima čestica se pomiče ispred glagola.
○	break out ➝ outbreak
○	put in ➝ input
○	spill over ➝ overspill

LANGUAGE FOCUS

===================================================

Expressing cause and effect

Linking phrases

●	Students feel they have no say in school affairs. As a result, they lose interest in school.

●	Students feel they have no say in school affairs, with the result that they lose interest in school.

●	The result of this is that they lose interest in school.

●	Students feel they have no say in school affairs, so that they lose interest in school.

A comment clause

●	Some students become disengaged from school. This has a negative effect on their behaviour.

●	Some students become disengaged from school, which has a negative effect on their behaviour.

A participle clause to express a result

●	Healthy schools promote the well-being of students. This results in improved learning outcomes.

●	Healthy schools promote the well-being of students, resulting in improved learning outcomes.

●	This leads to improved learning outcomes.

●	Healthy schools promote the well-being of students, leading to improved learning outcomes.

Writing

===================================================

A proposal

Title

●	Include a neutral title.

Introduction

●	Introduce the issue and say why it is important.

●	State the aim of the proposal.

Current situation

●	Present the current situation.

●	Provide any information needed to explain why you are going to recommend changes.

Recommendations

●	State your recommendations.

○	Recommendation 1: I would recommend that … should …

○	Recommendation 2: Another suggestion would be to …

○	Give reasons. (There may be more than two.)

○	Reason 1: This would enable/encourage … to …

○	Reason 2: This would mean that … could …

Conclusion

●	Summarise the benefits of your recommendations.

●	Persuade the reader to take the recommended action.

Proposal to promote healthy eating at school

●	INTRODUCTION

○	Healthy eating habits are important for young people’s growth, health and educational achievement.

○	In my view, the quality of food and catering facilities are currently unsatisfactory.

○	The purpose of this proposal is to identify the main problem areas and recommend improvements.

●	CURRENT SITUATION

○	We face several challenges when it comes to the catering facilities.

○	The canteen serves hot meals, but the food is poor quality and does not meet recommended nutritional standards.

○	The place itself is dark and unwelcoming and has long queues with the result that many students are discouraged from eating there.

○	Instead, they eat junk food such as sweets and salty snacks bought at nearby shops, which might have a negative effect on their ability to concentrate in class.

●	RECOMMENDATIONS

○	I propose that the school should adopt policies that regulate which foods can be served at school.

○	The canteen should offer more fresh produce, including salads, fruit and vegetables, and freshly made sandwiches.

○	The school shop should only sell snacks that are low in sugar, fat and salt.

○	This would ensure that students have access to healthy food during the school day.

○	I would also recommend refurbishing the canteen to create an attractive social and physical environment.

○	In this way, students will be less likely to go off-site for lunch where they might choose less healthy food options.

●	CONCLUSION

○	These recommendations will help to improve the health and academic performance of our students.

○	Therefore I urge the Head Teacher to take immediate steps to implement them.

Emphasising a piece of information
