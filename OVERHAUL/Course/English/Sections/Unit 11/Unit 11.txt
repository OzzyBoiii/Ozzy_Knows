● Spekulacija o sadašnjosti i prošlosti

    ○ Spekulacija o sadašnjosti
        ○ Kada nagađamo o sadašnjoj situaciji, koristimo modalni glagol + infinitiv
    ○ must
        ○ Izražava jako uvjerenje da je nešto istinito
        ○ ● John must be very happy working at the zoo
    ○ might, may, could
        ○ Koristimo kada mislimo da je moguće da je nešto istinito
        ○ ● The lions might be very hungry now
        ○ ● The lions may be very hungry now
        ○ ● The lions could be very hungry now
    ○ can't
        ○ Izražava jako uvjerenje da nešto nije istinito
        ○ ● It can't be a domestic cat
        ○ ● It's too big

    ○ Spekulacija o prošlosti
        ○ Kada nagađamo o prošloj situaciji ili događaju, koristimo modalni glagol + have + past participle glavnog glagola
    ○ must have
        ○ Izražava jako uvjerenje da se nešto dogodilo
        ○ ● You must have lost your mobile at the zoo
    ○ might have, may have
        ○ Kada mislimo da je moguće da se nešto dogodilo
        ○ ● Lucy might have missed the train
        ○ ● Lucy may have missed the train
        ○ ● Lucy could have missed the train
    ○ can't have, couldn't have
        ○ Izražava jako uvjerenje da se nešto nije dogodilo
        ○ ● Peter can't have gone home
        ○ ● Peter couldn't have gone home

● Used to i would (+ infinitiv)

    ○ Koristimo za govor o prošlim stanjima ili radnjama koje su se redovito događale u prošlosti
    ○ used to
        ○ Koristi se za stanja ili radnje
        ○ ● I didn't use to like travelling by plane
        ○ ● (ali sada volim)
        ○ ● She used to write travel guides
        ○ ● (Ali više ne piše)
    ○ would
        ○ Koristi se za radnje (ali ne za stanja) u potvrdnim rečenicama
        ○ ● When I was a teenager, I would visit my grandma every Sunday

    ○ (TABLICA 1)
        ○ Potvrdni oblik: I used to go backpacking every summer / I would go backpacking every summer
        ○ Niječni oblik: I didn't use to buy flight tickets online
        ○ Da/ne pitanje: Did he use to go on holiday in August?
        ○ Pitanja s upitnim riječima: What did he use to eat?
        ○ Pitanja s upitnom riječju kao subjektom: Who used to go to the seaside on holiday?

● Jezik

===================================================

● Frazealni glagoli (glagol + čestica/e)

    ○ Izrazi koji se sastoje od glagola i čestice, zahvaljujući kojima glagol dobiva novo značenje
    ○ ● look - look up (= provjeriti)
    ○ Većina frazealnih glagola ima svoje ekvivalente u formalnom jeziku
    ○ ● put up a tent = construct/erect a tent

    ○ Značenje frazealnih glagola
        ○ Doslovno
            ○ ● Why don't you take off your coat?
        ○ Idiomatsko
            ○ ● The plane takes off at 10 a.m.

    ○ Podjela frazealnih glagola
        ○ Nerazdvojni
            ○ glagol + čestica + objekt
                ○ Uvijek postoji objekt nakon frazealnog glagola
                ○ ● When I was running, I bumped into my form teacher
                ○ ● We dropped our bags and made for the passport control
            ○ glagol + čestica
                ○ Nema objekta nakon frazealnog glagola
                ○ ● Fortunately, a lot of people turned up to the exhibition
                ○ ● It was a long journey so we set off early in the morning
            ○ glagol + čestica + čestica + objekt
                ○ Uvijek postoji objekt nakon frazealnog glagola
                ○ ● I look forward to travelling around America next summer
                ○ ● It was very hot and we ran out of water very quickly
        ○ Razdvojni
            ○ Uvijek postoji objekt
            ○ Ako je objekt imenica, može doći prije ili poslije čestice
            ○ glagol + čestica + objekt
                ○ ● Can you please pick up Laura from the airport?
                ○ ● (Can you please pick up her from the airport - notirano kao primjer netočnog)
            ○ glagol + objekt + čestica
                ○ ● Can you please pick Laura up from the airport?
                ○ ● Can you please pick her up from the airport?

● Elipsa

    ○ Koristi se u neformalnom engleskom za izostavljanje riječi
    ○ Obično se izostavljaju subjektne zamjenice i pomoćni glagoli na početku rečenice kada je značenje očito
        ○ ● Having a great time
        ○ ● (I am having a great time)
        ○ ● Seems like five minutes ago
        ○ ● (It seems like five minutes ago)
    ○ Također se mogu izostaviti ponovljene riječi
        ○ ● Feeling a bit tired but I always am
        ○ ● (I'm feeling a bit tired but I'm always tired)

● Pisanje

===================================================

● Osobni email s davanjem savjeta

    ○ Započnite općim novostima i/ili referencom na ono što je vaš prijatelj napisao u posljednjem emailu
    ○ Izrazite suosjećanje s prijateljevom situacijom
        ○ ● I understand what a difficult decision it is
        ○ ● I can see why you're worried
        ○ ● I can see why you're unhappy
    ○ Ponudite savjet postavljanjem pitanja
        ○ ● Have you thought about ...
        ○ ● Why don't you ...
    ○ Ponudite savjet davanjem izjave
        ○ ● If I were you, I would ...
        ○ ● It's a good idea to ...
    ○ Promijenite temu i recite nešto pozitivno ili dajte više novosti
        ○ ● By the way, thanks for ...
        ○ ● Incidentally, did you hear that ...?
    ○ Uvjerite prijatelja na kraju emaila
        ○ ● I'm sure everything will be ok
        ○ ● I'm sure things will work out fine
