1. Cornell bilješke (digitalno u OneNoteu)
Otvri novu Cornell stranicu za lekciju (npr. “Antička književnost – Grčka / Rim”).
Gornji redak: upiši naslov i datum (kao i do sada).
Lijevi stupac: dok čitaš/slušaš, zapisuj samo najvažnije pojmove i teze koje kasnije želiš produbiti (razdoblja, žanrovi, autori, ključni pojmovi, pravila).
Desni stupac: umjesto “dugih objašnjenja” upisuj ono što se teško objašnjava čistim tekstom – skice, sheme, mini‑tablice, vremenske crte, slike/screenshotove, formule, primjere zadataka.
Donji dio: umjesto sažetka lijepi linkove na videe, članke i dodatne materijale koje ćeš koristiti za dublje učenje ili ponavljanje (npr. dobar YouTube o antici, PDF sa sažetkom, tvoj Quizlet).
Vrijeme po većoj cjelini: otprilike 45–75 minuta, jer digitalni Cornell bude brži od ručnog i ujedno ti već radi “organizaciju” gradiva umjesto study wheela.
2. Detaljnija obrada pojmova (uz Cornell + internet/udžbenik)
Prođi pojmove iz lijevog stupca jedan po jedan.
Za one koji su ti nejasni ili su “must know” (za ispit, eseje) otvori linkove iz donjeg dijela ili nakratko pretraži internet/udžbenik.
Dodatna objašnjenja piši i dalje u desni stupac, ali sažeto i svojim riječima – 1–3 rečenice ili kratka lista, nikakvi dugački referati.
Ako pronađeš odličan izvor (video, članak), dodaj link dolje da ga imaš za kasnije ponavljanje.
Vrijeme: 30–45 minuta po cjelini, ako se držiš samo zaista bitnih pojmova.
3. Konačna memorija uz Memory Palace (AI landscape)
S Cornell stranice izdvoji popis stvari za “čvrsto napamet”: liste (razdoblja, autori, djela, pravila), godine, definicije – 15 do max. 30 stavki po palaceu.
AI neka ti generira landscape (npr. antički grad, knjižnica, školski hodnik); ti samo odluči fiksni redoslijed 10–20 lokacija (ulaz, kip, fontana, most, hram…).
Za saku stavku napravi “ludu” sliku i zalijepi je na lokaciju (npr. Homer na ulazu viče stihove, Virgil gradi Rim na trgu, pravila naglaska ispisana na stupovima hrama).
Prođi rutu 3–4 puta u glavi, redom, sve dok ne možeš iz glave nabrojati sve informacije bez gledanja bilješki.
Vrijeme kao početnik: oko 20–40 minuta za 15–30 elemenata (poslije ćeš biti brži).
4. San / konsolidacija i kratka ponavljanja
Nakon ovakve sesije ne moraš odmah “legnuti spavati”, ali je super ako lekciju radiš u drugom dijelu dana i poslije imaš normalan noćni san 7–9 h – to pomaže da se činjenice stabiliziraju u dugotrajno pamćenje.
Sljedeći dan napravi brzi walkthrough kroz memory palace (5–10 min) i kratko aktivno ponavljanje iz Cornell bilješki: pokrij desni stupac, gledaj lijevi i pokušaj objasniti pojmove.
Nakon par dana ponovi isto (palace + Cornell), ali kraće; to je tvoja “spaced repetition” komponenta na vrhu metode loci.

Key Theory: Information as Data
All information starts as data—letters, numbers, symbols—which naturally form "nuclear" Memory Palace locations (e.g., Aristotle's alphabet focus). Books can become mini-palaces at advanced levels, but this foundational idea is often skipped.

Step 1: Organize Your Space
Select a familiar real location (e.g., your neighborhood or a bookstore) and create a logical journey between "Magnetic Stations" (key points along the path). Sketch it simply to plan—no need to memorize new locations, as this reduces cognitive load. Be flexible: assign info chunks variably (e.g., 17 words on one station or one syllable per station) for optimal recall.

Step 2: Encode with Magnetic Imagery
Weave new info into dynamic mental images using familiar elements from your mind. Match sounds/phonetics (e.g., "priggish misgivings" → Miss Piggy in a cafe). Encode out of order if needed—your brain will sequence it during recall. Think of it like musical notes: position evokes the sound.

Step 3: Elaborate Images
Amplify images for stickiness using models like KAVE COGS (check free training: magneticmemorymethod.com/mnemonic-images). Alternatives: Tony Buzan's SMASHN' SCOPE or Giordano Bruno's advanced conceptual encoding (e.g., On the Composition of Images). Practice deeply to build your system.

Step 4: Recall Rehearsal (Essential for Long-Term Mastery)
Don't just "store"—use spaced repetition with active recall (tutorial: magneticmemorymethod.com/spaced-repetition). Embrace the effort; studies show it strengthens memories. Avoid skipping steps early on.

Next-Level Advances
Master basics, then integrate Major System for numbers and 00-99 PAO (Person-Action-Object) for symbol systems. Combine into five mnemonic tools via Magnetic Memory Method.

This structured approach turns uncertainty into mastery—practice consistently for big learning projects like medical studies.

* ## 6 Key Scrolling Habits of High-Curiosity Minds

These patterns signal a brain foraging for intellectual value, not just dopamine.

- **Habit 1: Dissect comments for logic**  
    Dive into comment sections to spot fallacies, weak arguments, evidence gaps, and sharp insights. It's like a critical thinking gym—treats social media as a debate arena.
    
- **Habit 2: Hunt for cognitive value**  
    Skip shallow entertainment; seek explanations of complex ideas, behavior breakdowns, or perspective flips. Brain filters via headlines/thumbnails for "new info" potential (information foraging theory).
    
- **Habit 3: Rabbit holes**  
    A small detail pulls you deep (e.g., 45 mins from a clip to Wikipedia). Knowledge gaps create motivational pull—feels rewarding to close them, even without practical payoff.
    
- **Habit 4: Save/screenshot/open tabs**  
    Collect fragments (quotes, infographics) for future connections. Pattern thinkers hoard as "map sketches," not clutter.
    
- **Habit 5: Lightning-fast scan, deep dives**  
    Swipe rapidly to scan for high-value signals (unusual headlines/quotes), then invest heavily (full reads, source checks). Strategic foraging minimizes wasted attention.
    
- **Habit 6: Drawn to novel/counterintuitive**  
    Skip predictable content; pause on paradoxes or "brow-furrowers" that challenge your worldview. Novelty invites deeper thinking.
    ## Self-Improvement Actions

Turn these habits into tools for growth (e.g., integrate with your Anki/Obsidian setup):
