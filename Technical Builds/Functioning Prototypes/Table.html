Question Type Structure Examples
Yes/No Questions Am + I + verb-ing? Am I working now?
Are + you/we/they + verb-ing? Are you working now?
Wh- Questions Wh-word + do + I/you/we/they + verb? What languages do you speak?
Wh-word + does + he/she/it + verb? What languages does she speak?
Subject Questions Who + verb + ...? Who speaks English?
Negative Form Subject + am/is/are + not + verb-ing I am not working now.
Time Expressions now, at the moment, currently, today, this week
Special Cases Stative verbs (know, believe) don't use continuous form
