Implementation Guide

After downloading files, place them in your project folder and reference them locally

HTML Implementation
<!-- GIF Example -->
<img src="assets/gifs/space.gif" alt="Space animation">

<!-- Lottie Animation -->
<lottie-player src="assets/lottie/confetti.json" 
               background="transparent" 
               speed="1" 
               style="width: 300px; height: 300px;" 
               loop autoplay>
</lottie-player>

<!-- HTML5 Video -->
<video width="640" height="360" controls>
  <source src="assets/videos/nature.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>
