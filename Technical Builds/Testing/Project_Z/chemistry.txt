[SECTION:Trigonometry Basics]
Trigonometry deals with relationships between angles and sides of triangles.

Basic trigonometric functions:
- **Sine (sin)**: Opposite/Hypotenuse
- **Cosine (cos)**: Adjacent/Hypotenuse
- **Tangent (tan)**: Opposite/Adjacent

[FORMULA:Law of Sines]
For any triangle with sides a, b, c and angles A, B, C opposite those sides:

a/sin(A) = b/sin(B) = c/sin(C)

This relates sides to their opposite angles.

[EXAMPLE:Right Triangle Problem]
In a right triangle, if angle A = 30° and hypotenuse = 10, find the opposite side:

sin(30°) = Opposite/Hypotenuse
0.5 = Opposite/10
Opposite = 0.5 × 10 = 5

The opposite side is 5 units.

[SECTION:Calculus Introduction]
Calculus is the mathematics of change and motion.

Two main branches:
- **Differential Calculus**: Rates of change and slopes
- **Integral Calculus**: Accumulation of quantities and areas

[FORMULA:Derivative Definition]
The derivative of function f(x) at point x is:

f'(x) = lim(h→0) [f(x+h) - f(x)] / h

This represents the instantaneous rate of change.

[EXAMPLE:Basic Derivative]
Find the derivative of f(x) = x²:

Using power rule: d/dx(xⁿ) = n·xⁿ⁻¹
f'(x) = 2x

The derivative is 2x.
