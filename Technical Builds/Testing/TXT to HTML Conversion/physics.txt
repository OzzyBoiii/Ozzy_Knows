&PHYSICS - MECHANICS
Unit 1: Kinematics

Motion Equations:
v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as

Where:
v = final velocity
u = initial velocity
a = acceleration
t = time
s = displacement

§
Lab equipment needed:
- Motion sensors
- Timer
- Measuring tape

ˇEXPERIMENT: Free Fall
Objective: Measure g
Procedure:
1. Drop object from height h
2. Measure time t
3. Calculate g = 2h/t²
4. Repeat 5 times
5. Average results

Safety: Ensure clear drop zone

ˇPROBLEM SET:
1. Calculate force needed to accelerate
   5kg mass at 3m/s²
   Solution: F = 5 × 3 = 15N

2. Find acceleration of 10kg object
   with 50N force
   Solution: a = 50/10 = 5m/s²
