ˇLAB PROCEDURE: Titration
Materials:
- Burette
- Conical flask
- Indicator
- Standard solution

Steps:
1. Fill burette with acid
2. Measure base into flask
3. Add 2-3 drops indicator
4. Titrate until color change
5. Record volume used

Safety: Wear goggles, lab coat
