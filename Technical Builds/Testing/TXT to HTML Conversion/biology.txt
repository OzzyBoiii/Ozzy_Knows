&BIOLOGY - CELL BIOLOGY
Cell Theory:
1. All living things are made of cells
2. Cells are basic unit of life
3. New cells come from existing cells

Cell Types:
• Prokaryotic: simple, no nucleus
• Eukaryotic: complex, has nucleus

§
Microscope settings:
- 40x for overview
- 100x for detail
- 400x for organelles

ˇEXPERIMENT: Osmosis
Materials:
- Potato slices
- Salt solutions
- Balance
- Ruler

Procedure:
1. Cut equal potato pieces
2. Weigh each piece
3. Place in different solutions
4. Wait 30 minutes
5. Re-weigh and measure

Expected results:
- Hypertonic: shrinks
- Hypotonic: expands

Ribosomes: Protein synthesis
ER: Transport system
Golgi: Packaging center
